# My PG — Product Roadmap

A phased rollout ordered by development priority. Each section is a self-contained area of the product with its own spec, data, and screen designs.

## Sections

### 1. Owner Dashboard
The command centre for hostel owners — shows revenue this month, payment dues, vacant beds, unresolved tenant issues, and recent expenses at a glance. Default landing page after owner login.

### 2. Hostel & Room Management
Everything needed to set up and maintain a hostel profile and physical structure — floor plan builder, room and bed configuration, facility tagging, and room activate/deactivate controls.

### 3. Tenant Discovery
The mobile-first interface for prospective tenants to browse available hostels, view details, and initiate booking or onboarding.

### 4. Payments & Financials
Monthly rent reminder scheduling, operational expense logging (grocery, salaries, maintenance, fees), and a profit and loss report per hostel and per period. No payment gateway — reminders and manual confirmation only.

### 5. Issues & Community
Tenants raise maintenance or service issues with description and photo; owners view, assign, and resolve. Includes roommate group chat, hostel-wide community channel, and bidirectional review submission on checkout.

### 6. Sales Agent & Admin Panel
Internal growth tooling — super admin creates agents and areas, leads flow in from the website, agents manage their pipeline and track commissions, and admins monitor onboarding progress across the platform.
